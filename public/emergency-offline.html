Release now

  1. 1 · Stop and release

    Stop the activity. Support the person's position and release restraint without creating a fall or another injury. Use the practiced rapid-release method within your training.

  2. 2 · Call emergency services

    Call now for abnormal breathing, unresponsiveness, seizure, chest pain, severe bleeding, a serious fall, stroke-like symptoms, major weakness, or any neck pressure followed by concerning symptoms.

  3. 3 · Follow the dispatcher

    Follow dispatcher instructions. Give CPR, AED care, or first aid only within your current training. Do not use this app instead of emergency care.

  4. 4 · Prompt medical assessment

    Seek prompt care for persistent or worsening numbness, weakness, pain, swelling, discoloration, temperature change, breathing or swallowing change, voice change, confusion, dizziness, or loss of function.

  5. 5 · Do not re-tie

    Do not re-tie, delay care for a photo, or use normal-looking color or an app result to rule out injury.
